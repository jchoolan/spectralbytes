SPECTRALBYTES — OPERATION GHOST PACKET
======================================
STAGE S01 — FIRST SIGNAL

A suspicious transmission was recovered after the NullWave incident.

Your task is to investigate the evidence available in your home
directory and identify the first signal left by the attacker.

Useful commands may include:
    ls
    cat
    find
    grep
    file

Do not expect the answer to be stored in an obvious flag file.
The recovered evidence may contain information relevant to
subsequent stages.

Good luck, analyst.
