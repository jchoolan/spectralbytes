S03 — Nonce Once
=================

Telemetry-A contains a predictable routing record.

Known plaintext for telemetry-a:

ROUTE: S04-PCAP / GATEWAY-7 / incident-transfer-archive.zip / XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX

Use this known plaintext with ciphertext A to recover the reused AES-CTR
keystream segment. Then XOR that keystream with ciphertext B.

The AES key is intentionally unavailable.

Do not brute-force AES.
